How we protect your timeline and budget

48-hour approval window

When we deliver a process map, architecture blueprint or sprint module, you have 48 hours to approve or request revisions. No response is treated as approval, so delays on either side never stall the team.

Data readiness

Where a data migration, CRM rollout or data build is in scope, clean legacy exports are provided by the agreed kickoff date. Fragmented or late data pauses the timeline and is handled through a data-remediation change order.

Scope discipline

A request that falls outside the approved architecture is logged to a backlog and scoped as its own sprint, so the build you approved is the build you get.
